Frequently Asked Questions

Is FloatText free?expand_more
Yes! FloatText is free to download and use. An optional ad-free upgrade is available via a rewarded video — no subscriptions, no hidden fees.
What is the "Draw over apps" permission?expand_more
FloatText needs the Display over other apps permission to show the floating window above your active app. This is the only special permission required.
Does FloatText collect any data?expand_more
No. FloatText does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your text stays on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob under their privacy policy.
The floating window disappeared — what do I do?expand_more
Look for a small "F" bubble icon on screen edge — the window is minimized. Tap to restore. If completely gone, swipe down and tap the FloatText notification to re-open.
