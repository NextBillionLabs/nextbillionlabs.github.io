FloatText

Floating Clipboard & Quick Copy Tool

About FloatText

FloatText is a powerful floating clipboard tool that helps you copy multiple lines of text quickly using a floating window that stays on top of other apps.

Download on Google Play

Features

  • Floating clipboard window
  • Quick copy multiple lines
  • One-tap copy navigation
  • Floating bubble mode
  • Dark mode support
  • Fast and lightweight

Privacy Policy
